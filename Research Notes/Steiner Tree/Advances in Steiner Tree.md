## Summary
- The book is divided into 2 sections:
	- First part's about Steiner Tree in plane (in $\mathcal{L}^d_{p}$ ): locate some unknown Steiner points to minimize connection cost, theoretical analysis: Exact algorithms, efficient solution for special case, estimation on the bounds
	- Second part's about SPG: Steiner problem in Graph: Given graph $G(V,E)$, the sets Terminal and Steiner are known beforehand, compute Steiner Minimal Tree.
